# Presentation Order

The presentation order follows the chronological order in which new ways of handling MWEs in both Linguistics and NLP appear:

- **Linguistic Definition:**
    - Nunberg et al. (1994)     - what do we call idioms, what are the criterions ?
    - Sag et al. (2002)         - are MWEs hard to process in NLP and why ?
    - Baldwin et al. (2010)     - what are the common considerations between linguistics and NLP regarding MWEs ?
    

- **NLP processing:**
    - Constant et al. (2017)    - what is the taxomony of MWE processing in NLP ?
    - Fort et al. (2020)        - how can we annotate MWEs in NLP ?
    - Pearce (2001)             - task and methodology (MWE discovery)
    - Pasquer et al. (2020)     - task and methodology (rule-based identification)
    - Constant et al. (2011)    - task and methodology (CRF identification)
    - Boukobza et al. (2009)    - task and methodology (Bi-LSTM identification)
    - Taslimipoor et al. (2020) - task and methodology (BERT identification)
